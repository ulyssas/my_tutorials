There are 3 types of bones that control legs:

- Leg (FK): controlled by IK
- Leg IK: (at the feet) controls FK leg chain
- Leg D: extra FK adjustment when using IK (does not affect IK)

In MMD, there's no convenient feature like FK-IK snapping, so even if you turn off IK or use Leg D, Leg IK bone won't move. You should use Leg IK Parent for adjusting motions, or use Rigify Integration in [MikuMikuRig](https://github.com/XiaoFFGe/MikuMikuRig) / [MMD Tools Append](https://github.com/MMD-Blender/blender_mmd_tools_append) (so you can use FK-IK/IK-FK snapping).

Additionally, you can use [MotionSupporter](https://bowlroll.net/file/233713) to convert leg FK motions into leg IK.
