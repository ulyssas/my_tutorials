1. Open TransformView then disable all IK.
2. Select the bone you want to scale.
3. Enter the number in the box and click the arrow button to apply to all axes.
4. Click `Scale` to scale. Do this for other bones as well.
5. Click `Update Model -Curent Deformed State-` to apply the edit.
