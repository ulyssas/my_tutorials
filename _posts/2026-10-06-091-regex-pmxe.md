If you are using PmxEditor 0273, you can use something called [RegEx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_expression). You can put RegEx pattern into the box and Shift-click to select the bones that match the condition. (This works for other tabs as well)

{% include discord-gallery.html id="gallery" %}

In this example, `裙子_[0-9]_4` means the bones with prefix `裙子_`, suffix `_4` and number 0-9 in between. After selecting the bones, you can delete them easily.

The change log of 0273: [https://kkhk22.seesaa.net/article/490064244.html](https://kkhk22.seesaa.net/article/490064244.html)
